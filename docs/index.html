What the model read

Before reading, numbers are spelled out in Hindi and English words are removed, the same way the training data was prepared. Crossed-out words are not in its vocabulary, so it skips them. Faded words come after its 300-word limit.

How it works

The model is an encoder-decoder Transformer with 4 layers, 10 attention heads and 300-dimensional fastText Hindi word embeddings. It was trained on articles from the ILSUM 2023 dataset and writes the summary one word at a time, always choosing the most likely next word, up to 75 words.

For this page, the trained weights were exported to ONNX and run with ONNX Runtime Web. They are stored as 16-bit numbers to halve the download to 40 MB. Compared with the original model, this changes a few words in about 7 of every 100 test summaries.

What it gets wrong. It was trained from scratch on a small dataset, so it often mixes up names and numbers, sometimes repeats itself, and only knows the most common words from its training articles. In the same project, a fine-tuned IndicBART did much better: a ROUGE-1 F-score of 0.55, against 0.39 for this model.
